# Spotter — what it does

> "Even trainers need a Spotter"

A plain-English tour of everything in the app, and answers to the questions that come up.
**The app:** https://drewdavistraining-coach.github.io/spotter/

---

## The four tabs

| Tab | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Clients** | Everyone you train. Tap a name for their profile, timeline and progress. |
| **Week** | Your whole week across every client: who's training which day, what's logged, what's cancelled. |
| **Drills** | Your drill library — the pool the weekly planner builds from. |
| **Settings** | Your name for recaps, session types, weight unit, sync, backups. |

---

## Clients

**Adding a client** (+ Client, top right) sets up:

- **Name, email, phone** — the email is where their recap goes.
- **Goal** — shows at the top of their profile ("First amateur fight in March").
- **Program levels** — Off / Beginner / Intermediate / Advanced in Boxing, Muay Thai, Jiu Jitsu, Wrestling,
  MMA, Strength and Conditioning. This is the heart of it: the planner only ever picks drills at or below
  their level in that discipline. A client can be Advanced boxing and Beginner jiu jitsu.
- **Usual training days** — which days the auto-builder fills.
- **4-week cycle started** — where they are in the training block (see below). Change it to line the cycle
  up with reality.
- **Rating categories** — what you score 1–5 each session. Trim it to what they actually train: a
  cardio-and-mobility client doesn't need a takedowns score.
- **Background notes** — injuries, experience, schedule. Private to you.

**Their profile page** shows the goal, discipline levels, where they are in the 4-week cycle, and four
buttons: Log session, Voice memo, Week plan, Recap. Below that: **Timeline** and **Progress**.

## The weekly program (the part that saves you the guesswork)

Each client runs a repeating **4-week block**:

1. **Learn** — new technique, clean reps, low intensity.
2. **Build** — more volume, combinations, partner pressure.
3. **Apply** — pressure-test it: live and situational rounds.
4. **Test & recover** — lighter week. Rate every skill; the next block adapts.

Every block puts **one discipline up front**, and that focus rotates block to block (a boxing block, then a
Muay Thai block, and so on). That's what stops a long-term client drifting into whatever you're training
yourself, and it means next week is never a blank page.

**Auto-build** fills the week from that. It favours drills they haven't done in the last few weeks, drills
that train skills rating low, drills at their level, and the intensity the phase calls for. A drill harder
than the week calls for is unlikely to appear — no hard sparring in a Learn week.

**The Week tab has "Auto-build all"**, which plans every client without a plan in one tap.

### Working on a week plan

- **+ Drill** — add from the library (filtered to their level) or type a custom one.
- **⠿ grip** — hold and drag to reorder, or drag onto another day.
- **↑ ↓** — move a drill up or down; at the top or bottom of a day it hops to the next day.
- **⇄** — swap for a different drill of the same type at their level.
- **🏋️** — plan the weights: sets of weight × reps. It shows what that client last lifted on that drill,
  with a **Repeat** button. The icon turns orange once a drill has planned weights.
- **✕** — remove, with **Undo** for a few seconds.
- **🚫 Cancel** — mark the day cancelled (see below).
- **The bar at the top** shows the mix of categories, whether anything repeats from the last 2 weeks, and
  whether the week covers what they need work on.

## Logging a session

- **Date, type, minutes.** The Type list is yours to edit — "+ New type…" adds one on the spot.
- **Drills** — one tap adds everything planned for that day ("From this week's plan"), or add from the
  library, or type your own.
- **Weights** — tap a drill (or the 🏋️) for weight × reps rows. **+ Set** copies the set above.
  Strength drills open automatically, showing last session's numbers and a **Repeat** button. If the week
  plan prescribed weights, those come across already filled in.
- **Ratings 1–5** for that client's categories. Tap the same number again to clear it.
  **Edit categories** changes their list without leaving the screen.
- **What went well / Work on next** — these go into their recap.
- **Private coach notes** — only you ever see these.
- **Voice memos** — record straight onto the session.

**If a client cancels:** either tap 🚫 Cancel on that day in the week plan (with a reason: illness, injury,
work, travel, no-show…), or use "🚫 Client cancelled this session" at the bottom of the log screen. The day
turns amber, keeps its plan so you can move it, and can be undone. Cancellations never count as training,
but they do show in their timeline, progress and recap.

## Voice memos

Record per client, or attached to a session. Each memo has a note box under it; tap the mic key on the
iPhone keyboard to dictate into it rather than typing. Memos and private notes are **never** included in a
client's recap — they're shown to you on the recap screen as reference while you write.

## Timeline and Progress

**Timeline** is everything for that client in order: sessions (with drills, weights, ratings and notes),
voice memos, cancelled days, and recaps you've sent. Tap a card to open it. The 🗑 on any card deletes it,
with **Undo** for a few seconds.

**Progress** shows:

- **Areas to sharpen** — their weakest categories by recent ratings, which the planner leans into.
- **Skills** — a trend line per category, the current average, and an arrow for improving / steady / slipping.
- **Sessions per week** for the last 8 weeks, with cancellations in amber, plus what percentage of booked
  sessions they actually trained.
- **Weights** — each drill's latest sets, top set, and how it compares with the first time you logged it.

When a discipline's ratings have averaged 4+ for a month, their profile suggests **moving them up a level**.

---

## Recaps

Pick a date range (last 7 / 14 / 30 days, or since the last recap) and Spotter drafts the email:

- sessions trained, with the drills
- anything cancelled
- weights, with the change since last time
- rating changes versus the period before
- what's working, and areas to sharpen
- next week's plan, including planned weights
- your closing line and name

Your private notes for that period sit above the draft for reference and stay out of the email. Edit
anything, then **Open in Mail** hands it to your mail app with the client's address filled in — you hit send.
Sent recaps are saved to their timeline.

## Drills

The 92 starter drills are a starting point. **Make them yours**: the planner is only as good as this list.

- **✎** renames a drill in place. The new name carries into this week's and future plans; past weeks keep
  the old name as history.
- Tap a drill to edit its **category, level, intensity, default dose, the skills it trains, and coaching cues**.
- **+ Drill** adds your own. Search and category filters sit at the top.

Level and intensity are what the planner reads: level decides who gets the drill, intensity decides which
week of the block it belongs in.

## Settings

- **Your name and closing line** for recap sign-offs.
- **Weight unit** — lb or kg. Changing it doesn't convert numbers already logged.
- **Session types** — the Type dropdown when logging.
- **Sync** — sign in to keep phone and laptop matched (below).
- **Backup** — save a file copy of everything.
- **Install on iPhone** and storage protection.

---

## Sync, offline and backups

- **Sign in on each device** (Settings → Sync). Changes show up on the other device within seconds when
  both are online.
- **No signal at a gym?** Keep working. Everything saves on the phone and uploads when you reconnect;
  Settings shows how many changes are waiting.
- **Same thing edited on both devices** before they sync: the most recent edit wins.
- **Your data is private to your account.** Nothing is shared with anyone, and clients have no login.
- **Backups** (Settings → Back up now → Save to Files) are an extra copy you control. Worth doing
  occasionally even with sync on.

---

## The four rules behind everything

Most surprises make sense once you know these:

1. **The newest edit wins.** Two devices editing the same thing? Whichever was saved last is what you keep.
2. **Plans and sessions keep their own copy.** Once a drill is in a week plan or a logged session, it holds
   its own name and numbers. Changing the library later doesn't rewrite it.
3. **History is never rewritten.** Past weeks, logged sessions and sent recaps stay as they were.
4. **Nothing is really gone until the Undo bar disappears.** You get 12 seconds on anything deleted from a
   client's timeline, and about 7 on a drill removed from a week plan or a session you're logging. Deleting
   something else replaces the bar, so only the most recent delete can be taken back.

## Cause and effect

| What you see | Why | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| An edit you made on the phone isn't on the laptop | The phone hadn't synced yet — no signal, or the app wasn't opened | Open Spotter on the phone and wait for Settings → Sync to say "Up to date" |
| An edit seems to have been undone | The same record was edited on both devices; the later save won | Redo it on one device and let it sync before editing on the other |
| Settings says "3 changes will upload" | You're offline; the work is saved on the device | Nothing. It uploads when you reconnect |
| A drill you deleted came back | Another device still had it and synced afterwards | Delete it once both devices are signed in and up to date |
| You renamed a drill, but an old week still shows the old name | Past weeks keep the name they were planned with | Nothing — that's the history being accurate |
| You deleted a drill from the library, but it's still in a week plan | Plans keep their own copy | Remove it from the plan itself with ✕ |
| A client's progress chart changed after you deleted a session | Charts and recaps are built from sessions, so its ratings and weights went too | Tap Undo, or log the session again |
| A cancelled day doesn't count as a session | Cancellations are deliberately kept out of training stats | Nothing — it still shows in the timeline, attendance % and recap |
| A rating category disappeared from a client | It was removed from their list | Add it back — its past ratings return with it, nothing was lost |
| Weights show the wrong unit | Switching lb ↔ kg only changes new entries; old numbers aren't converted | Re-enter the ones that matter, or switch the unit back |
| This week is the wrong phase of the block | The phase is counted from "4-week cycle started" on their profile | Change that date to line the cycle up |
| Auto-build keeps picking drills that are too easy or too hard | It reads the client's level in that discipline, and each drill's own level | Adjust the client's levels, or the drill's level in the Drills tab |
| You changed a client's level but this week didn't change | Existing plans stay as they are | Auto-build the week again, or edit it by hand |
| The session shows different weights than the plan | Planned weights are a prescription; the session records what really happened | Nothing — that's the point |
| A session type you deleted still shows on an old session | Sessions keep the type they were logged with | Nothing |
| "Spotter is open in another tab or window" | Two copies are open on a laptop and an update needs them closed | Close the other tab; the page carries on by itself |
| The app doesn't have a change you just published | The phone hasn't picked it up yet | Close it fully and reopen, sometimes twice. Or tap "Spotter was updated" |
| You erased the device and everything came back | You're signed in, so it re-downloaded from your account | Expected. To clear the cloud too, ask for help first |
| You restored an old backup and newer entries are still there | With sync on, restoring merges instead of replacing; newest wins per record | Expected |
| Sync says "Signed out — please sign in again" | The sign-in expired | Sign in again; your data on the device was untouched |

## What's changed since the first version, and what it means

| Change | What it means for you |
|---|---|
| **Sync added** | Phone and laptop stay matched. A backup file is no longer the only way to move data — but restoring one now merges instead of replacing |
| **Updates arrive on open** | No more waiting several opens for a change; if you're mid-screen you get a "tap to refresh" button instead of losing what you typed |
| **Levels renamed** | "Foundations" is now "Beginner". Nothing about your clients changed |
| **Rating categories per client** | Each client is scored only on what they train. Removing a category hides it, keeps its history |
| **Session types editable** | Add your own types as you log, or manage the list in Settings |
| **Rename drills in place** | The ✎ button. New name flows into this week and future weeks; past weeks keep the old one |
| **Cancellations** | A cancelled day is a record, not a gap. It keeps its plan, stays out of training stats, and appears in the recap |
| **Weights per set** | Logged per drill per client, so the Progress tab and recaps can show what changed. Plans can prescribe weights that carry into the session |
| **Drag and drop, arrows, Undo** | Reorder a week by dragging a grip, or with ↑ / ↓ at the edges to hop days. A mis-tapped ✕ is recoverable |
| **Delete from the timeline** | Any card can be removed, with Undo. Deleting a session also removes its ratings and weights from the charts |

## FAQ

**A client cancels — should I delete the day?**
No. Mark it cancelled. You keep the record and the reason, it stays out of their training stats, and the
plan for that day is kept so you can shift it.

**I deleted something by mistake.**
Tap **Undo** in the bar at the bottom of the screen. It's there for several seconds after any delete in the
timeline or the week plan.

**A drill I deleted came back.**
That happens when another device still had it and hadn't synced yet. Delete it once both devices are signed
in and it stays gone.

**Why is the planner ignoring a drill?**
Usually its level is above that client's level in that discipline, or they did it in the last couple of
weeks. Check the drill's level in the Drills tab.

**Can I plan weights but log different ones?**
Yes — that's the point. The plan holds the prescription; the session records what actually happened. Editing
the session never changes the plan.

**Does the client see any of this?**
Only the recap email you choose to send. Not your private notes, not your voice memos. A client login is a
future idea, not built.

**How do I move a whole day's work to another day?**
Drag each drill by its grip onto the other day, or use ↑ / ↓ at the edge of a day.

**The app didn't update.**
Close it fully and reopen. If something still looks stale, open it once more.

**New phone?**
Install Spotter, sign in, and everything downloads.
